The clinging plants found the joints

    Still to prove: that a stem in a gap wedges it is taken from what clinging plants are known to do to fences and cladding; the simulator does not show it and the plant library does not source it. The splayed lap is set out and cut in the model, not proved. Its sizes are provisional, nobody has checked what it does to the strength of the lap, and how thick a clinging stem gets after ten years is not in the plant library. The simulator reports where a clinging stem arrives and nothing about what it then does to a joint, so it cannot test the new cut.
